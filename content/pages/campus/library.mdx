## More than books

Our librarians teach research skills to every year group, run book clubs from Year 2 to Year 13 and curate a growing collection of Indian and world literature in English, Hindi and French. Digital subscriptions give Sixth Formers access to journals and archives.

## Reading mentors

Older girls train as reading mentors and spend two lunchtimes a week reading with younger pupils. It is one of the most popular volunteering roles in the school.
