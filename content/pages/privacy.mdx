## Who we are

Aurelia Hall School is operated by Aurelia Hall Educational Trust (a fictional entity). We are the data fiduciary for personal data processed through this website and our school systems. Contact our Data Protection Officer at the placeholder address on the contact page.

## What we collect and why

We collect only what we need for a specific purpose (purpose limitation), and we tell you the purpose at the point of collection.

* **Enquiries and tour bookings** — your name, phone, email and details about your child, so we can respond and arrange visits.
* **Registration and admission** — information about your child and family, documents you upload and assessment results, to process the application.
* **Enrolled pupils** — academic, pastoral, medical (restricted access) and financial records needed to educate and care for your daughter.
* **Job applicants** — information in the staff application form, for safer recruitment.
* **Website analytics** — only if you consent through the cookie banner.

## Children's data and parental consent

We process children's personal data only with verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and only for the child's education and wellbeing. We do not track children for advertising or build behavioural profiles of children.

## Who can see your data

Access is role-based. For example, medical information is visible only to authorised medical, boarding and senior staff. Every access to sensitive actions is logged.

## Retention

We keep data only as long as needed: enquiries that don't proceed are deleted after 24 months; applicant data after 12 months if the child does not join; pupil records for the period required by law after a pupil leaves. Retention periods are configurable by the school.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. School staff process export and deletion requests through an auditable workflow in our systems. You may also raise a grievance with us, and with the relevant authority.

## Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in and protect forms. Analytics and advertising cookies are off by default and load only if you opt in; you can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, access is logged and staff are trained in data protection.
