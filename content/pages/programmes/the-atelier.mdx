## Projects we love (examples)

A rain-gauge network for the kitchen garden; adjustable easels for a local art class; a bilingual board game for Pre-Prep; and a small business selling upcycled tote bags whose profits fund the library's new graphic-novel shelf.

## Enterprise

Sixth Formers can run a real micro-venture through The Atelier, with a budget, a mentor from our enterprise partner network (sample) and a stall at the Kesarbagh Saturday market.
