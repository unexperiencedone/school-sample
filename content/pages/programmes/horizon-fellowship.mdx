<Lead>The Horizon Fellowship takes girls from Year 9 to Year 13 through a ladder of challenges: serving the community, surviving the hills and, finally, leading something that matters.</Lead>

<Cards items={[
  { title: "Bronze · Year 9", text: "A term of weekly community service, a two-day hike and a first-aid qualification." },
  { title: "Silver · Year 10–11", text: "A four-day expedition, a skills project and a leadership role in a club." },
  { title: "Gold · Sixth Form", text: "A self-designed capstone: a service project, research study or venture with real impact." }
]} />

## Why it matters

Leadership isn't a badge. It's what happens when you are tired, it's raining, the map is wet and someone in your group is struggling. The Fellowship gives girls those moments — safely, with expert guides — and the time to reflect on what they learned.

## Safety first

All expeditions are run with qualified instructors, a staff ratio of at least 1:6 and full risk assessments shared with families in advance.
