## How we teach it

IGCSE courses begin in Year 10 and are examined at the end of Year 11. Before that, our Key Stage 3 curriculum (Years 7–9) is designed to build exactly the skills IGCSE rewards: analysis, structured writing, mathematical fluency and confident practical science.

## Choosing subjects

In Year 9, every girl meets her tutor and subject teachers to choose options. Everyone takes English, Mathematics and the sciences; then she adds subjects she loves and subjects that keep doors open. Families are part of every conversation.
