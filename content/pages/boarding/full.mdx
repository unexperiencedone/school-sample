## The houses

Banyan, Kingfisher, Saffron and Tamarind each have a houseparent family living in, a deputy and a team of tutors. Younger boarders share rooms of four; Sixth Formers have single study-bedrooms. Every house has a kitchen, a common room, a garden and a lot of board games.

<Split image="/images/feature-boarding.webp" title="A day in the house" alt="A boarding house common room">Wake-up at 6:45, breakfast together, lessons, sport and clubs. Prep is supervised from 6:30 to 8:00 pm, then free time, house meetings, a call home and lights-out by age.</Split>

## Weekends

Saturday mornings are for fixtures, music and clubs. Afternoons and Sundays bring trips — hikes, markets, museums, the lake — and plenty of time to rest. Boarders may go home on exeat weekends, twice a term.

## Staying close to home

Boarders call home every evening. Parents receive a weekly house newsletter and can reach the houseparent any time. We celebrate birthdays, festivals and the small victories in between.
