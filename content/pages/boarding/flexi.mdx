<Lead>Flexi boarding lets girls join house life for part of the week and go home for the rest. Many families use it as a bridge into full boarding; others love it as it is.</Lead>

## How it works

Choose a regular pattern of two, three or four nights a week (Monday to Thursday) at the start of each term. Your daughter has her own bed and storage in a house, the same houseparent team and the same evening programme as full boarders.

  { title: "Regular nights", text: "A fixed pattern each term, so friendships and routines can settle." },
  { title: "Extra nights", text: "Book occasional extra nights through the parent portal, subject to space." },
  { title: "Easy transitions", text: "Move to full boarding at the start of any term." }
]} />

## Fees

Flexi boarding is charged per night pattern each term. See the full-boarding fee page for the current sample rates and how they appear on your invoice.
