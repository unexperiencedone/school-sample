<Lead>Aurelia Hall began with a question asked around a kitchen table: what would a girls' school look like if we designed it today, for the world our daughters will actually live in?</Lead>

The founding families (fictional, like everything on this sample site) found their answer on a forgotten hillside estate outside Kesarbagh: a stone hall with a long arched cloister, an overgrown orchard and a field that seemed to run straight into the sky. The hall gave us our name — *aurelia*, golden, for the way the morning sun fills the arches.

The school has grown, but the founding question hasn't changed. We still ask it every year, and we still change things when the answer does.
