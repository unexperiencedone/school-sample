Dear families,

Thank you for finding your way here. Choosing a school is one of the most hopeful decisions a family makes, and I want this page to help you make it well — even if the answer turns out not to be us.

Aurelia Hall is an all-girls day and boarding school for ages four to eighteen, set on fourteen acres of hillside above the town of Kesarbagh. We follow the British curriculum, from the Early Years through IGCSE and A Level. But a curriculum is only a map. What matters is how a school walks it with each child.

We walk it slowly enough to notice. Our classes are small, so every teacher knows every girl's name — and also how she likes to learn, what makes her laugh, and which subject she secretly loves. We set high expectations because we believe in our girls, and we give them the support to meet them because believing isn't enough on its own.

<Pull by="From our founding charter">Ambition and kindness are not opposites. We teach them together.</Pull>

You'll find our days full: lessons that ask big questions, a field that's never empty after half past three, a theatre that's rarely quiet and four boarding houses that feel, on a good evening, like the warmest version of home. You'll also find room for stillness — the library terrace at dusk, the walk back from the kitchen garden, the five minutes after lights-out when a houseparent stops by to say goodnight.

The best way to know whether Aurelia Hall is right for your daughter is to visit. Come to an Open Morning, or book a private tour. Ask our girls anything; they will tell you the truth.

With warm wishes,
