<Lead>In a girls' school, girls aren't the exception in any room. They are the physicists, the captains, the coders, the lead in the play and the Head Girl — every time.</Lead>

## Confidence without competition for airtime

Research on single-sex education is mixed on results, but consistent on one thing: girls in girls' schools are more likely to choose subjects like physics, computer science and further mathematics, and more likely to say they feel confident speaking up. We see it every day.

<Split image="/images/gallery-lab.webp" title="Science is for her" alt="A science lab bench">Our girls take physics and computer science at twice the national rate (sample figure). Not because we push them, but because nobody ever told them it wasn't for them.</Split>

## Designed around how girls grow

Our wellbeing curriculum, our approach to sport and our pastoral care are designed with girls' development in mind — from building body confidence to navigating friendships online.

## Ready for a mixed world

All-girls doesn't mean sheltered. Our girls debate, compete, perform and collaborate with boys' and co-educational schools throughout the year, and leave ready to lead in any room they walk into.
