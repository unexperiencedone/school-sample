## Teaching that is built on evidence

Our teachers plan lessons around what research tells us works: retrieval practice, worked examples, regular low-stakes quizzes, feedback that's specific and kind, and time to think before anyone answers. Every teacher spends two hours a week in professional development, including observing colleagues.

## Excellence beyond the exam hall

Excellence is a habit, not a grade. We celebrate the girl who rewrites her essay four times, the team that loses well, the cast that holds a scene together when a line goes missing. Results follow from habits; we teach the habits.

## Support for every learner

Our learning support team works with girls who need extra scaffolding and with those who need extra stretch. Individual learning plans are reviewed each term with families.
