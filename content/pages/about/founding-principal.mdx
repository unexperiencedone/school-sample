<Note>Dr. Helena Varghese is a fictional person created for this sample site. Her portrait is an illustration.</Note>

<Lead>Helena has spent twenty-five years in classrooms on three continents. She founded Aurelia Hall to build the school she always wished she could send her own students to.</Lead>

Before Aurelia Hall, Helena taught English and led two international schools, where she was known for walking the corridors at lunchtime and remembering every pupil's name. She holds a doctorate in education, focused on how adolescent girls build academic confidence, and she still teaches a Sixth Form literature seminar every week.

<Pull by="Dr. Helena Varghese">A head who stops teaching stops listening. Mondays, I'm in the classroom.</Pull>

## In her words

"I believe schools should be places of high challenge and low fear. Girls do their best thinking when they feel safe enough to be wrong out loud. So we build a culture where asking a question is brave, where effort is noticed, and where every adult knows that their job is to help a young person become more fully herself."

## Outside school

Helena runs the hill path most mornings, grows far too many tomatoes in the kitchen garden, and is working, slowly, through every novel on the Sixth Form reading list.
