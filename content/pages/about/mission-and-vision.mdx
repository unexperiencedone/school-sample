<Lead>We exist so that every girl leaves Aurelia Hall knowing three things: what she cares about, what she's capable of, and how to be good to the people around her.</Lead>

## Our mission

To give girls an education of real depth — rigorous, joyful and humane — in a community where each one is known, challenged and cared for, so that she can lead a life of purpose and use it well.

## Our vision

A school whose graduates are recognised not only for their results but for their judgement, their kindness and their nerve: young women who ask better questions, take on hard problems and lift others as they rise.

## How we hold ourselves to it

Every year we ask pupils, parents and staff the same twelve questions about belonging, challenge and care, and we publish what we learn — including the parts we need to improve. Values only count if you can see them in an ordinary Tuesday.
