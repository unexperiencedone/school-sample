<Lead>We want Aurelia Hall to be open to girls with the curiosity and drive to thrive here, whatever their family's means.</Lead>

<Note>Scholarship values and criteria are sample figures. They are configured in the CRM's fee engine as concessions, so the numbers you see on fee pages always match.</Note>

## Academic scholarships

Up to **50% of tuition** for girls joining Year 7, Year 9 or Year 12 who show exceptional academic promise. Awarded after a scholarship paper and an interview with the Principal. Scholarships are reviewed annually and continue while a girl keeps working hard.

## Specialist scholarships

Up to **25% of tuition** in music, sport, art and design, or drama, awarded after an audition, trial or portfolio review. Specialist scholars take on leadership in their area.

## Means-tested bursaries

Bursaries of up to **100% of fees** for families who could not otherwise afford them, assessed confidentially on household income and assets. Bursaries can be combined with a scholarship.

## Sibling and founding-family concessions

A **10% tuition concession** for the second and subsequent daughters attending at the same time, and a **5% concession** for founding families. These are applied automatically on your invoice — no application needed.

## How to apply

1. Register your daughter as usual and tick "I'd like to be considered for a scholarship or bursary".
2. Our admissions team will send you the relevant form and assessment dates.
3. Decisions are communicated with the offer letter.
