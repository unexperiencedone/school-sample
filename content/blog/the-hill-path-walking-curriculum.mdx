Behind the Long Field, a narrow path climbs through the trees to a rocky outcrop with a view over three valleys. We call it the hill path, and every class walks it at least once a term.

## The same path, different eyes

Reception count the steps (it's 1,214, though no two counts agree). Year 3 map it. Year 6 measure the gradient. Year 9 survey the plant species for the Ridge Conservation Project (sample). Year 12 geographers study erosion. Sixth Form leaders take Pre-Prep buddies up on their first walk.

Over fourteen years, a girl who joins in Nursery will walk it more than forty times.

## Why repetition matters

Walking the same place over and over teaches noticing: the season the tamarind fruits, the year the landslip changed the bend, the way the view looks in the monsoon. It's one of the quieter ways we teach girls to pay attention to the world.

## Join us

Families are welcome on the hill path walk at every Open Morning. Wear sensible shoes.
