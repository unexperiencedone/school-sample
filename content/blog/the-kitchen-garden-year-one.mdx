A year ago, the patch of land behind the dining hall was an overgrown orchard. Today it's a kitchen garden with eighty raised beds tended by every year group.

## By the numbers (sample figures)

- **300 kg** of vegetables sent to the dining hall
- **12** varieties of chilli (a Year 8 obsession)
- **1** rain-harvesting system designed by Atelier students
- **0** bananas, thanks to the monkeys

## What the girls learned

Pre-Prep learned that seeds need patience. Prep learned about soil, worms and compost. Upper School studied water use and built drip irrigation. Sixth Form economists costed the whole operation and concluded that, as a business, it is a delightful failure — and as an education, a great success.

## What's next

This year we're adding a herb garden for the boarding houses and a "pick your own" Friday for day pupils. The monkeys have been notified.
