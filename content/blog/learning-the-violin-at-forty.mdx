When we launched family music lessons last year, we expected a handful of sign-ups. Thirty-one families joined.

One of them was a mother and her nine-year-old daughter (both fictional, for this sample), who decided to start the violin together on the same day.

## "She's better than me, and she knows it"

Within a month, the daughter was ahead. She practised more, worried less and found it hilarious when her mother's bow squeaked. The mother says the role reversal was the best thing that's happened to their relationship this year.

## What adults remember about learning

Our music teachers say adult beginners bring something valuable into the room: they remember that learning something new is hard. Children watching their parents struggle — and persist — get a powerful lesson in what effort looks like.
